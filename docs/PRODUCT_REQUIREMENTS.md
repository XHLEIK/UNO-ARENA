# UNO ARENA — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Executive Summary
UNO ARENA is a modern, real-time, zero-friction multiplayer UNO gaming platform. Players can create rooms, send instant invite links or 6-digit codes to friends, choose a nickname without registration, and play authentic Classic UNO on desktop or mobile.

## 2. Target Audience & Core Experience
- Friends and families looking to play together instantly.
- Casual card gaming groups without app store installs or signups.
- Mobile and desktop web browsers with low-latency responsiveness.

## 3. Key Feature Scope
1. **Instant Anonymous Entry**: No password, phone number, or email required. Cryptographic session tokens with reconnect recovery.
2. **Room Management**: 2 to 10 players, configurable room codes, host controls, ready-up status.
3. **Official Classic Rules**: 108 cards, correct starter card behavior, skip, reverse (2-player variant), draw two, wild, wild draw four with challenge rule, UNO call and catch window.
4. **House Rule Modules**: Optional stacking rule toggled by host.
5. **Two Scoring Modes**: Quick Play (Single Round) and Tournament (Cumulative Scoring with target points).
6. **Grace Period & Bot Takeover**: When a player disconnects, an automated bot plays legal moves until reconnection.
7. **Vector UNO Card Design**: Responsive, authentic SVG/CSS vector rendering with accurate symbols, colors, corner pips, and fanned hand layout.
